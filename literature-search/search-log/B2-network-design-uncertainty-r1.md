## Transfer notes for the spaceport-to-orbit feeder problem
- Reliable location (1, 4, 12, 15): orbital hubs/depots that are intermittently unreachable (phasing windows, outages) map onto facility-failure models with backup-assignment levels; Lagrangian relaxation and continuum approximation give scalable heuristics.
- Hub location under uncertainty (6, 10, 11, 16, 17): choosing which orbits/stations are hubs and allocating spaceports under uncertain demand and hub set-up costs; scenario-based SP, minimax regret, budget-of-uncertainty RO and adaptive DRO are the available formalisms, with branch-and-cut / Benders solution machinery (7).
- Stochastic service network design (2, 8, 9): launch schedule as a periodic time-space network; recourse via rerouting payload or acquiring/outsourcing vehicles; consolidation at hubs as a hedge against demand uncertainty.
- Two-stage robust network design (3) and p-robust network design (5): bound worst-case or regret of the feeder plan when manifests shift.
- Decomposition (13, 14): partial Benders and Benders dual decomposition are the exact methods to scale scenario-based launch network design.
