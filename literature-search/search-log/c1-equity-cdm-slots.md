## Method lineages and transfer to RAM flow problem (for the writer)
- CDM / RBS / compression / slot trading (#1, #2, #11, #13, #18, #19, #20, #21, #32): single-resource slot timeline at one airport (or AFP sector); flight-to-slot assignment IP; RBS is the schedule-order (proportional) baseline, compression / mediated bartering re-optimise within airline-declared substitutions or at-least/at-most offers; stochastic variants (#18 dual-network structure, #19 scenario tree, #20 equity+strength) integrate into CDM GDPs; #2 replaces schedule order by distance priority; #11 is the airline-side slot reallocation response (DP + B&B, rolling horizon). For RAM: departure/arrival/charger slots at regional airports and vertiports, multiple operators (scheduled + on-demand), short electric legs absorb late information (RBD logic).
- Network fairness in ATFM IPs (#3, #4, #24): fairness metrics (deviation from FSFS, reversals, overtaking) embedded in the Bertsimas–Stock Patterson 0-1 time-expanded model; exponential penalties; a second slot-reallocation stage for airline collaboration. Directly importable into a RAM time-expanded flow model with corridor/airspace-block capacities.
- Inter-airline equity and user preferences in rerouting/TBO (#13, #35, #36): IP choosing one trajectory/option per flight with airline preference or equity terms (AFP slot exchange, CTOP/UDPP-style trajectory options, data-driven preference extraction).
- Scheduling-level demand management and slot allocation (#5, #6, #7, #14, #16, #17, #27, #28, #29, #34, #37, #10): displacement-minimising IPs under declared capacity with IATA priority classes, inter-airline fairness (proportionality indicators, bi-objective, lexicographic/stage mechanisms), passenger-centricity, network-wide connectivity; coupled with stochastic queuing / two-stage stochastic programming to size caps (#5, #16, #37) and with game-theoretic airline frequency responses (#27, #10). Strategic layer ahead of the tactical RAM flow problem; closest analog for allocating launch windows / pad slots among spaceport users (Topic B).
- Market mechanisms and pricing (#8, #9, #30, #33, #12, #31): analytical welfare comparisons of congestion pricing vs slot sales/trading/auctions under profit motives and uncertainty; credit/compensation-based ATFM slot market with IR, budget balance, Pareto efficiency; voting/ranking aggregation of airline inputs with gaming analysis; price-of-fairness bounds for proportional and max-min fairness. Templates for pricing/trading slot or charger capacity among RAM operators and for launch-window allocation.
- Classical deterministic/stochastic ground-holding foundations seen as by-catch (#15, #22, #23, #25, #26, #38).
