## Transfer notes (methodology -> spaceport-to-orbit feeder design)
- Express SND (Barnhart & Schneur 1996; Kim et al. 1999; Armacost et al. 2002): many origins feed few hubs within hard sort windows on a time-space network; candidate aircraft routes with capacity; aircraft count/balance. Composite variables bundling route+covered demand give strong LP bounds -> "launch mission" variables bundling spaceport, window, vehicle, manifest.
- Air cargo network planning (Derigs et al. 2009; Zhu et al. 2026): integrated flight selection, aircraft rotation, cargo routing with through-connections via column generation -> integrated launch selection, vehicle rotation, payload routing.
- Fleet assignment / rotation / maintenance routing (Rushmeier & Kontogiorgis 1997; Barnhart et al. 1998; Rexing et al. 2000; Gopalan & Talluri 1998; Talluri 1998; Liang & Chaovalitwongse 2013; Haouari et al. 2013): time-space networks with ground arcs, turnaround minima, cyclic rotation tours with periodic maintenance -> reusable booster rotation with refurbishment cycles.
- Integrated schedule design + fleet assignment with demand interactions (Lohatepanont & Barnhart 2004; Pita et al. 2013; Wei et al. 2020; Birolini et al. 2021; Yan et al. 2022): choose which legs/windows to open and vehicle type with spill/recapture -> payload deferral across windows.
- Hub-and-spoke vs direct economics (Lederer & Nambimadom 1998; Hansen 1990; Hong & Harker 1992; Dobson & Lederer 1993; Adler 2005): when hubs beat direct service -> how many orbital depots vs direct-to-target.
- Hub connection timing and sort capacity (Trietsch 1993; Fedtke & Boysen 2017): buffer/ground-time newsboy models and hub throughput design -> orbital hub transfer windows and berthing capacity.
- Time-definite / lead-time constrained design (Lin 2001; Lin & Chen 2004; Greening et al. 2023): service commitments as hard path constraints -> orbital insertion deadlines.
- LTL load plans with limited flexibility / dynamic routing (Jarrah et al. 2009; Erera et al. 2013; Baubaid et al. 2021, 2023): next-terminal rules and p-alt flexibility -> payload transshipment rules at hubs under uncertainty.
