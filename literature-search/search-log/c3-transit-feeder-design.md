## Transfer notes for the writer
- Continuum-approximation hub-and-spoke / trunk-feeder sizing (Daganzo 2010; Badia 2014; Ouyang 2014; Chen 2015; Fielbaum 2016; Nourbakhsh-Ouyang 2012; Wirasinghe 1977; Hurdle 1973): closed-form trade-off between feeder density/headway and hub (trunk) capacity — first-cut sizing of spaceport catchments and launch cadence.
- Fixed vs flexible feeder policy thresholds (Chang-Schonfeld 1991 TS; Quadrifoglio-Li 2009; Li-Quadrifoglio 2010; Kim-Schonfeld 2014; Chen-Nie 2017 x2): critical demand density for switching between scheduled and on-demand feeder launches.
- Timed transfer / hub synchronization under stochastic delay (Hall 1985; Bookbinder-Désilets 1992; Fonseca 2018; Abdolmaleki 2020; 2023 TR-B dwelling times): slack-time and synchronization IPs for feeder arrivals meeting orbital-hub departure windows.
- Schedule-based/time-expanded line planning and large-scale column generation (Steiner-Irnich 2018 TS; Bertsimas-Ng-Yan 2021 OR; Pinto 2020 TR-C; He 2024 TR-C): exact/matheuristic machinery for timetabled feeder services with discrete departure slots.
- Access-node location and pricing (Wang-Yang-Lindsey 2004 TR-B): spaceport (access node) siting with user mode-choice equilibrium.
